Privacy

This website does not run analytics, advertising trackers or a customer database. Its enquiry form builds a WhatsApp message in your browser; the website does not receive or store the information you enter.

When you contact us

WhatsApp is a third-party service. Opening it or sending a message is subject to WhatsApp’s privacy terms. Information you choose to send may be used by Asili Spices to respond to your enquiry and discuss an order. Avoid sending sensitive personal or payment information.

External services

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives the network information needed to serve them. Hosting providers may process technical request logs to deliver and secure the site. External links take you to services with their own privacy practices.
