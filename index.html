Our story

“Asili” means going back to what is real.

The name Asili speaks to origin, nature and authenticity. It captures a simple idea: ingredients should retain their identity, and flavour should feel honest rather than overcomplicated.

Asili Spices brings that philosophy into the kitchen through an evolving spice range created for everyday meals, memorable recipes and professional food use. The brand is shaped by curiosity, care and a respect for the way good ingredients can transform food.

Meet the founder

Where scientific curiosity meets a love for authentic flavour.

Ann Kaaria is a Kenyan researcher and scientist and the founder of Asili Spices. Her professional identity brings a naturally thoughtful, evidence-aware mindset to the brand — one that values observation, consistency, learning and careful attention to detail.

Asili Spices reflects a broader vision: to build a trusted Kenyan brand around natural ingredients and authentic food experiences while remaining open to innovation, customer feedback and responsible growth.

“Pure. Natural. Authentic.” is the idea at the centre of Asili Spices — a simple standard for how the brand wants people to experience flavour.

Frequently asked

How do I place an order?

Call or WhatsApp Asili Spices on +254 722 658559. We can confirm current products, pack sizes, availability and ordering details.

Do you supply businesses?

Trade enquiries are welcome from retailers, restaurants, caterers, hospitality businesses, institutions and resellers. Availability and terms can be discussed directly.

What products are currently available?

The range may evolve over time. WhatsApp us for the most current catalogue rather than relying on an old product list.

How should spices be stored?

Keep spices tightly sealed, dry and away from direct sunlight or strong heat. Use clean, dry utensils when scooping to help preserve aroma and quality.

Can I ask about a spice that is not listed here?

Yes. The website highlights representative categories, not a fixed inventory. Contact us with the exact spice or blend you need.
